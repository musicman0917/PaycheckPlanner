# 💰 Paycheck Planner

A free, single-file budgeting tool built for the **NeighborhoodofMusic** community. No accounts, no subscriptions, no data sent anywhere — everything runs entirely in your browser.

---

## Features

### Budget Tab
- Add recurring expenses organized by **vendor and category** (housing, transport, subscriptions, etc.)
- Set each bill's **frequency** (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly) and it converts everything to a per-paycheck cost automatically
- Add **one-off payments** with due dates — the planner flags which ones fall in your next paycheck window
- Track your **savings account balance and APY** to see estimated monthly interest earned
- Calculates exactly how much to **transfer to savings** each paycheck to cover all your bills, with the remainder shown as free spending money in checking

### Debt Snowball Tab
- Add all your debts with balance, minimum payment, and interest rate
- Enter an **extra monthly payment** amount and watch the snowball math work in real time
- Debts are automatically sorted **smallest balance first** — classic snowball method
- See the **🎯 Target** debt highlighted at all times
- When a debt is paid off, its minimum payment automatically rolls forward to the next target
- Summary cards show **Total Debt**, **Months to Freedom**, and **Total Interest Paid**
- Side-by-side comparison of payoff date and interest saved **with vs. without** your extra payment
- **Monthly Roadmap table** — expand to see exactly what to pay on every debt, every month, until you're debt-free

## How to Use

1. Open `Debt Tracker and Snowball.html` in any modern web browser — no installation required
2. Fill in your income and bills on the **Budget** tab
3. Switch to the **Debt Snowball** tab and add your debts
4. Your data saves automatically as you type

---

## ⚠️ Important: Data is Local to Your Device

This tool saves your data using your browser's **localStorage** — meaning:

- Your data **never leaves your device** and is never sent to any server
- Data is saved **per browser, per device** — if you open it on your phone, it won't have the data from your laptop
- Clearing your browser's site data or cache **will erase your saved information**
- To avoid losing your data, **use the same browser on the same device** consistently

If you want to move your data to a new device, the simplest option is to keep the tab open or bookmark it and avoid clearing browser data.

## Running It

Since this is a single HTML file, you can use it several ways:

**Option 1 — Direct (easiest):** Just download `index.html` and open it in your browser. Double-click the file or drag it into a browser window.

**Option 2 — GitHub Pages:** If you've forked this repo, enable GitHub Pages under Settings → Pages and it'll be live at `https://[your-username].github.io/[repo-name]/`.

**Option 3 — Local server:** If you prefer running it through a local server (e.g. VS Code Live Server), that works fine too.
